Film Aging over Time

Simple Improvement as Z-Scores over Time

This figure was able to show the critical improvement of the collection of movies from 2023, though it doesn't account for popularity, and so wanted to create a visualization that would account for that

The Film Arc (Animated)

Notice how below you can see what direction the films went in both the increase in popularity and also their critical reception. What I did to section and group these films was if their rate of change in popularity was greater than the rate of change in the critical reception, then those are the two "popular" groups where you can see our orange region is the most typical path (mostly popular but decreasing or regressing to the mean critical scores), but our blue group is exactly the best group you would want to be in (increasing reception and better increases in popularity. Whereas you can then imagine the red group is exactly the worst place to be, where not many more people have seen your movie and the critic score decreased much more in proportion; but, the green region are our critical darlings, where not many more people have seen it but the critical response was outpacing the popularity increase.
